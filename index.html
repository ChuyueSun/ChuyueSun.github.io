01 / About

Hello, I’m Livia.

I am a Founding Member of Technical Staff at &Truth. I received my PhD from the Computer Science Department at Stanford University in June 2026, advised by Professor Clark Barrett. My research is published as Chuyue Sun.

Before Stanford, I studied EECS at MIT and worked with Professor Adam Chlipala in the Programming Languages and Verification Group. I have also interned at Microsoft Research.
